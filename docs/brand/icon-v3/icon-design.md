# TheRSS Icon v3 Design Brief

## Direction

Replace the circular v2 aperture with a non-letterform soft-corner diamond while preserving the abstract research-signal system.

## Mark Construction

- a broad warm-paper open diamond with visibly diagonal outer and inner contours;
- a small mustard index marker near the upper-right opening;
- one red signal circle near the lower-right opening;
- a deep-forest rounded-square field with transparent outer corners.

The rounded rhombus creates an aperture and directional flow without reading as `C`, `O`, `Q`, a page, or a literal RSS symbol.

## Preserved from v2

- Forest: `#183F34`
- Paper: `#F2EEE5`
- Mustard: `#F0C44F`
- Signal red: `#D84A2F`
- flat vector-like geometry;
- subtle dry-ink tactility;
- no text, letters, paper object, AI motif, gradient, glow, or depth.

## Generated Candidate

- File: `assets/brand/therss-icon-v3.png`
- Format: PNG, 1024 x 1024, RGBA
- Transparency: genuine alpha outside the forest squircle
- Small-size check: the diagonal aperture, yellow marker, and red signal remain separate at 64 x 64
- Generation path: built-in image generation, using v2 as the edit target, followed by a transparent-corner correction

## Final Prompt Summary

Change only the large warm-paper aperture from a circular C-like ring into an open rotated-square or rhombus outline with generous soft corners, visible diagonal contours, a right-side gap, and custom endpoints guiding attention toward the red signal. Preserve the forest field, mustard marker, red circle, restrained palette, print tactility, spacing, and transparent outer corners. Avoid every letterform, document metaphor, literal RSS arc, and AI visual convention.

## Delivery Boundary

v1 and v2 remain available. Do not integrate v3 into Electron packaging or the installed application until the user approves the direction.
