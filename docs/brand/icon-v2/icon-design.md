# TheRSS Icon v2 Design Brief

## Direction

Move from an editorial paper object to an independent abstract symbol.

## Core Metaphor

**Signal aperture:** an open geometric frame filters a field of information and holds one selected signal at its opening.

The symbol should suggest focus, filtering, discovery, and forward movement without literally depicting a document, inbox, brain, robot, or broadcast feed.

## Shape System

- one warm-paper-colored open aperture or bracket;
- one small mustard index block;
- one signal-red circle;
- deep-forest rounded-square field;
- negative space is part of the mark, not empty decoration.

The central glyph should use no more than three foreground shapes and must survive at 32 px.

## Invariants from v1

- Forest: `#183F34`
- Paper: `#F2EEE5`
- Mustard: `#F0C44F`
- Signal red: `#D84A2F`
- restrained editorial tone;
- subtle printed tactility;
- transparent outer corners.

## Remove from v1

- paper or document silhouette;
- clipped-page corner;
- text-like horizontal rules;
- folder or inbox reading;
- literal content representation.

## Avoid

- dominant RSS arcs;
- radar sweeps or crosshairs;
- eyes or camera-lens realism;
- letter monograms that need explanation;
- AI clichés, gradients, glow, glass, chrome, 3D depth, and tiny detail.

## Delivery Boundary

Save v2 separately from v1. Do not integrate it into Electron packaging or the installed application until the direction is approved.

## Generated Candidate

- File: `assets/brand/therss-icon-v2.png`
- Format: PNG, 1024 x 1024, RGBA with transparent outer corners
- Small-size check: the aperture, index block, and red signal remain distinct at 64 x 64
- Generation path: built-in image generation using v1 only as a palette and texture reference, followed by transparent-corner correction

## Visual Reading

- Primary: an open aperture holding one selected signal;
- Secondary: a subtle sense of curation, attention, and forward movement;
- Removed: document, page, inbox, and text-line readings.

## Final Prompt Summary

Create an abstract signal-aperture brand mark using exactly three foreground elements: a large warm-paper open frame, a short mustard index element, and one red signal circle. Center the glyph on the existing forest field, use negative space and confident custom geometry, and preserve restrained print tactility. Exclude paper, documents, literal RSS arcs, letterforms, AI motifs, gradients, glow, depth, and tiny details.
