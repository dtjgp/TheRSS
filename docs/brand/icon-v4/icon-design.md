# TheRSS Icon v4 Design Brief

## Direction

Restore an immediately recognizable paper metaphor while keeping the restrained, low-AI-feel visual system developed in v2 and v3.

## Mark Construction

- one warm-white research paper sheet with soft corners;
- one large, clean diagonal top-right fold or clipped corner;
- one mustard index marker behind the fold;
- one red selected-signal seal overlapping the right edge;
- a deep-forest rounded-square field with transparent outer corners.

The paper remains intentionally blank. Its meaning comes from its silhouette and the colored discovery markers, not from text-like lines or office-document detail.

## Meaning

- Paper: a research artifact, paper, note, or repository record ready for reading;
- Mustard marker: indexing and daily discovery;
- Red circle: the item selected as worth the researcher's attention;
- Forest field: calm, local-first, durable research work.

## Removed

- open diamond and letter-like aperture;
- writing lines, typography, and literal page content;
- folder, envelope, inbox, realistic curl, shadow, or layered 3D paper;
- AI clichés, RSS arcs, gradients, glow, and glossy effects.

## Generated Candidate

- File: `assets/brand/therss-icon-v4.png`
- Format: PNG, 1024 x 1024, RGBA
- Transparency: genuine alpha outside the forest squircle
- Small-size check: paper, fold, yellow index, and red signal remain legible at 64 x 64
- Generation path: built-in image generation, using v3 as the edit target, followed by a transparent-corner correction

## Final Prompt Summary

Replace only the warm-white open diamond with one simple research-paper sheet: a broad vertical page with soft corners and one clean diagonal top-right fold, indicated only by geometry. Preserve the forest field, mustard index marker, red signal circle, restrained palette, print tactility, and transparent outer corners. Exclude writing lines, office-document detail, realistic paper effects, RSS arcs, and AI visual conventions.

## Delivery Boundary

v1-v3 remain available. Do not integrate v4 into Electron packaging or the installed application until the user approves the direction.
